How to Search Multiple Job Platforms at Once (Without Losing Your Mind)

Published August 4, 2026 · 5 min read

If you're job hunting right now, you already know the routine: open LinkedIn, type your role, filter by location. Close it. Open Indeed, type the same role, filter by location again. Close it. Open Naukri. Repeat. By the time you've checked five sites, you've typed the same search five times and you're not even sure which listings you've already seen.

There's a faster way to do this, and it doesn't cost anything. Here's how.

Why searching one platform at a time doesn't work anymore

No single job site has every opening. Some companies only post on LinkedIn. Others only use Indeed or a regional site like Naukri. Startups often post exclusively on niche boards. If you're only checking one or two platforms, you're missing a large chunk of what's actually available.

The problem is that checking all of them manually is slow. Every platform has its own search bar, its own filters, its own login. A search that should take 30 seconds ends up taking 20 minutes.

The fix: search once, get links to everywhere

Instead of retyping your search on every site, you can use a tool that takes your role, skills, and location once, and builds ready-to-open search links for every major platform at the same time — LinkedIn, Indeed, and others, all pre-filled with your exact search.

You click each link, land directly on the real results page for that platform, and start applying. No extra typing, no separate logins to set up a search, no guessing which site to check next.

A simple 3-step routine

  1. Enter your role and skills once. If you have a CV/resume, some tools can scan it and pull your skills out automatically, so you don't have to type them by hand.
  2. Set your location and work mode. Remote, on-site, or hybrid — narrow it down once instead of on every individual site.
  3. Open each platform link in a new tab. Go through them one by one with results already filtered and ready.

Do this once a day, not once a week

New listings get buried fast, especially on high-traffic platforms. Running this kind of multi-platform search daily, even for five minutes, means you see fresh postings before hundreds of other applicants do.
